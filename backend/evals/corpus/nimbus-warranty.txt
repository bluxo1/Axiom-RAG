Nimbus Laptop Limited Warranty

Every Nimbus laptop carries a limited hardware warranty lasting twenty-four
months from the original purchase date. The warranty covers manufacturing
defects in the battery, the display panel, and the keyboard assembly.

Accidental damage such as cracked screens from drops, liquid spills, and damage
from unauthorized repairs is not covered by the limited warranty. An optional
accidental-damage plan may be purchased within sixty days of buying the laptop.

Battery capacity that falls below seventy percent of its rated charge within the
warranty period is treated as a defect and qualifies for a free battery
replacement. Shipping costs for a warranty repair are paid by Nimbus.

To start a warranty claim, the customer must provide the serial number printed
on the underside of the laptop and a proof-of-purchase receipt.
