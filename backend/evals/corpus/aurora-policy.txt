Aurora Home Insurance Policy — Coverage Summary

The Aurora homeowner policy covers accidental water damage caused by burst
plumbing pipes and by flooding from storm surge. Claims for burst-pipe damage
must be filed within thirty days of discovery. The standard deductible for a
water-damage claim is five hundred dollars.

The policy explicitly excludes damage caused by gradual seepage, by poor
maintenance, and by earthquakes. Earthquake protection is available only as a
separate rider purchased before the event.

Wildfire damage to the main dwelling is covered up to the full rebuild value
stated on the declarations page. Detached structures such as sheds and fences
are covered up to ten percent of the dwelling limit.

Temporary living expenses, including hotel and meal costs, are reimbursed for up
to twelve months while the insured home is uninhabitable after a covered loss.
